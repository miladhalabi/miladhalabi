### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Full-Stack & Embedded Systems Engineer** with extensive experience architecting end-to-end solutions spanning hardware firmware, real-time edge computing, industrial telecommunications, and modern full-stack web and mobile platforms.

- 🏢 **Technical Leadership:** Head of Software Department at **HUEC / Halabi Company**, directing software engineering operations, system architecture, and enterprise automation platforms.
- ⚡ **Embedded Systems & IoT:** Real-time firmware development, edge computing architectures, sensor matrices, and resilient offline-first edge engines.
- 🌊 **Field Engineering:** Field maintenance & repair of submarine telecommunications infrastructure (officially recognized for technical execution) and industrial telecom power distribution systems.
- 🔄 **Distributed Systems:** Architect of local-first data convergence, real-time state synchronization, and event-driven communication pipelines.
- 🖨️ **Prototyping & Workflows:** Custom structural enclosure fabrication, automated development environments, and advanced AI engineering workflows.
